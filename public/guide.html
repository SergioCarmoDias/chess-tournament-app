Tournament System Guide

Official user manual for managing your chess tournaments seamlessly using dynamic plans and automated round generation.

1. Tournament Overview & Plans

The system automatically selects the optimal tournament structure based on your total registered player count:

  • Plan A (≤ 6 players): Single Round-Robin Group (everyone plays everyone).
  • Plan B (7–12 players): Single Swiss System Group.
  • Plan C (13+ players): Two Batches (Swiss) for Stage 1, followed by Top 4 Playoffs/Round-Robin for Stage 2.

2. Player Registration & Batches

Navigate to the Registration tab to add participants:

  • Manual Entry: Type a player's name and click add.
  • CSV Upload: Upload a .csv file containing a column of player names to bulk-import competitors.
  • Batch Locking: Once all players are registered, locking batches divides participants into groups if your participant count triggers Plan C.

3. Pairings & Recording Results

Head over to the Pairings page to control rounds:

  • Generate upcoming round matches with a single click once all current round matches are finalized.
  • Record match outcomes immediately using the dropdown options: 1-0 (White wins), 0-1 (Black wins), or 0.5-0.5 (Draw).

4. Playoffs & Stage 2

Once preliminary rounds conclude without pending matches, the Playoffs section unlocks:

  • Top qualifiers from respective batches are automatically paired into Semi-Finals and Finals.
  • Track final standings and tournament winners directly from the podium page.

5. Tie-Break Calculations

Rankings are determined dynamically using standard chess tie-breaking metrics:

  • Points: Win = 1, Draw = 0.5, Loss = 0.
  • Sonneborn-Berger (SB): Sum of the scores of opponents whom a player has defeated, plus half the scores of opponents with whom they have drawn.
  • Buchholz System: Sum of the total scores of each of the player's opponents.
